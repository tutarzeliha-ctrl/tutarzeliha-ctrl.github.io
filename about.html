About Me

From e-commerce operations to analytics engineering

My story

I spent more than eight years in e-commerce: first as an E-Commerce & Sales Specialist at Parça Penye, then running my own online store for baby products and clothing, Zeye Bebek. Orders, stock, returns and campaign reports were part of my daily work.

Since 2020 I have been teaching myself SQL, Python and the modern data stack, and since August 2025 I have been building independent data projects. My main project is a real-time CDC lakehouse: PostgreSQL changes flow through Debezium and Kafka into DuckDB and Parquet, dbt builds tested models on top, and Airflow checks that the data is fresh and correct.

I am looking for an Analytics Engineer or Data Engineer role, remote across Europe or on-site in Switzerland and Monaco.

How I work

  • Test before trusting. Each change comes with a check that proves it works.
  • Say what was verified. My project docs separate what I tested from what I have not tested yet.
  • Start from the business question. Metric definitions come before models.
