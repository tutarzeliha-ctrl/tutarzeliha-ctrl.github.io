The Problem

SaaS company losing 15-20% of customers annually due to lack of churn visibility:

  • No early warning - Churn discovered after customer leaves
  • Reactive approach - "Win-back" campaigns are ineffective
  • $500K+ annual loss - 20% of 500 customers at $50K/year ARR
  • No segmentation - Cannot identify high-risk patterns
  • Manual outreach - Sales team doesn't know who to contact

The Solution

Built an ML-powered churn prediction pipeline that identifies at-risk customers 2 weeks in advance, enabling proactive retention campaigns via Reverse ETL.

ML Pipeline Workflow
Weekly Execution (Every Monday 9 AM):
1. Extract customer data from data warehouse
2. Engineer features from subscription + usage data
3. Load trained XGBoost model from registry
4. Predict churn probability for all customers
5. Classify into risk tiers: High (>80%), Medium (50-80%), Low (<50%)
6. Push segments to CRM via Reverse ETL
7. Send Slack alert with top 10 high-risk customers
8. Log predictions for monitoring & retraining

Key Learnings

ML Engineering
  • Feature importance: NPS trend > payment delays > support tickets
  • Class imbalance: SMOTE resampling improved minority class
  • Model drift: Monitor accuracy monthly, retrain quarterly
  • Business threshold: 70% prob = most profitable retention point
Operational
  • Reverse ETL critical: Predictions only valuable if actionable
  • Sales adoption: Require dashboard, not just API integration
  • Calibration: Bucket customers into "saved" or "false positive"
  • Feedback loop: Actual churn labels for model improvement
