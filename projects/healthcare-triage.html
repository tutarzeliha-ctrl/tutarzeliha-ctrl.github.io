Healthcare Triage Risk Scoring

Sensor Data → Prefect Orchestration → Real-Time Alerts

95% Accuracy

The Problem

In emergency healthcare settings, monitoring patient vital signs generates massive data streams that can overwhelm medical staff. Delays in identifying critical deterioration patterns can lead to severe patient risks and delayed medical intervention.

The Solution

An automated real-time healthcare triage pipeline powered by Prefect orchestration that ingests continuous patient sensor telemetry, computes risk scores with high clinical accuracy, and triggers instant alerts to medical staff via Slack.

Architecture Flow

Patient Sensor Streams → Prefect Orchestration & ML → Slack Real-Time Alerts

Key Engineering Highlights

  • Robust Orchestration: Built resilient data pipelines and workflows managed seamlessly using Prefect.
  • Clinical Risk Scoring: Processed continuous biometric feeds to classify patient deterioration risks with 95% accuracy.
  • Instant Notificationing: Integrated automated webhooks to push high-priority risk alerts directly to clinical response teams.
  • High Availability: Designed with fault-tolerant error handling to guarantee uninterrupted patient monitoring.

Business Impact

Faster Emergency Response: Empowers medical personnel with instant risk stratification, drastically reducing response times for critical patients and improving clinical outcomes.
