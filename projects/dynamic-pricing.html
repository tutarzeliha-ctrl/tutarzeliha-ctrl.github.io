E-Commerce Dynamic Pricing Engine

Real-Time Pricing Pipeline → ML Optimization

Real-Time Optimization

The Problem

Static pricing models in e-commerce fail to respond quickly to market fluctuations, competitor price changes, and shifting demand elasticity, resulting in missed margin opportunities or lost sales conversion.

The Solution

An automated dynamic pricing engine built to evaluate competitor pricing telemetry and demand signals in real time, executing optimization algorithms to compute and update competitive product prices instantly.

Architecture Flow

Competitor Scrapers / Kafka → Python ML Engine → Automated Price Updates

Key Engineering Highlights

  • Real-Time Telemetry: Ingested continuous market and competitor feeds to track price positioning.
  • Elasticity Modeling: Developed automated pricing rules and ML algorithms to maximize revenue and margin.
  • Automated Execution: Deployed safe execution pipelines to push updated prices directly to e-commerce storefronts.
  • Guardrails & Alerts: Implemented boundary constraints and anomaly detection to prevent extreme pricing shifts.

Business Impact

Revenue & Margin Growth: Maximizes profitability by dynamically capturing high-demand windows and maintaining competitive edge without manual oversight.
